Evolving Economy System

Found in the base preset ("tetris scav mode" / "Tetris stable") via the "ExponentialEvoEcoConTurV2" tweak by tetrisface — not something we built, reference/study only. Adds 3 families of exponentially-scaling buildings, directly buildable in any tier from any T2/T3 constructor (no forced build-up chain): Evo Fusion (30 levels), Evo Energy Converter (24 levels), and Construction Turret (30 levels, this is the "evo build turret with 30 variants" users see on T2 constructors). All 3 factions use identical math, just different base units/icons.
